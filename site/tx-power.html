Текст

THE MAIN ENGINE AS A HEAT ENGINE

A marine power plant converts the chemical energy of the fuel into work at the propeller. The main engine is a heat engine: air is drawn into the cylinder and compressed, fuel is injected into the hot air and burns, and the expanding gas pushes the piston down. The connecting rod turns this motion into the rotation of the crankshaft. Only part of the heat of combustion becomes useful work; the rest leaves the engine with the exhaust gas and with the cooling water. Engineers therefore draw up a heat balance of the plant, and every improvement of the engine is in fact an attempt to move heat out of the losses and into the work.

Обратите внимание на цепочку страдательных сказуемых: air is drawn … and compressed, fuel is injected. Второе причастие compressed присоединено к тому же is, поэтому глагол не повторяется. Слово the rest заменяет уже названное («остальная часть теплоты»), а in fact значит «на самом деле». Оборот an attempt to move heat — инфинитив в роли определения к существительному: «попытка перенести теплоту».

TWO CYCLES AND TURBOCHARGING

Two working cycles are in use at sea. In a four-stroke engine the piston makes four strokes, that is two revolutions of the crankshaft, for one power stroke. In a two-stroke engine the cycle is completed in a single revolution, so the same cylinder gives twice as many power strokes. Large two-stroke engines are slow-speed: they run at about a hundred revolutions per minute and are coupled directly to the shafting. Medium-speed four-stroke engines run several times faster and drive the propeller through a reduction gear. Almost every modern engine is turbocharged. The exhaust gas drives a turbine, the turbine drives a compressor, and the compressor forces scavenge air into the cylinder. The more air the cylinder holds, the more fuel can be burnt in it.

Вводное that is («то есть») выделено с двух сторон запятыми и поясняет предыдущее сочетание. Сравнение twice as many power strokes строится с исчисляемым существительным, поэтому взято many, а не much. Последнее предложение — двойной сравнительный оборот the more … the more: «чем больше воздуха, тем больше топлива». Обратите внимание и на предлог: двигатель is coupled to the shafting, а не «with».

THE SHAFTING AND THE PROPELLER

The power leaves the engine through the shafting. The thrust bearing takes the push of the propeller and passes it to the hull; the intermediate shaft carries the torque along the shaft tunnel; the stern tube supports the tail shaft where it passes through the shell and keeps the sea water out. At the end of the shafting a fixed-pitch propeller or a controllable-pitch propeller turns the torque into thrust. No propeller is fully efficient: it leaves a wake behind it, and its blades cavitate if the pressure on their back falls too low. As a rule, a large propeller turning slowly is better than a small one turning fast.

Три однородных предложения разделены точкой с запятой — обычный приём технического описания, когда перечисляют части одного узла. Оборот keeps the sea water out — глагол с послелогом: «не пускает забортную воду внутрь». В последней фразе a small one заменяет a small propeller, чтобы не повторять существительное, а причастия turning slowly и turning fast работают как русские причастные обороты.

EFFICIENCY AND ALTERNATIVES

In a modern slow-speed diesel engine about half of the heat of the fuel becomes work at the crankshaft; this figure is called the thermal efficiency. Part of the remaining heat is recovered in an exhaust gas boiler, which raises steam for the services of the ship. The diesel engine is not the only choice. A steam turbine or a gas turbine gives a light plant for its power but burns more fuel. In a diesel-electric plant the engines drive generators and electric motors drive the propellers, so the engines can be placed anywhere in the hull and can be stopped one by one when the load falls. Dual-fuel engines burn liquefied natural gas as well as oil.

Сказуемое is called вводит термин: «эту величину называют эффективным КПД». В придаточном с which запятая обязательна, потому что оно добавляет сведения о котле, а не выделяет его среди других. Сочетание as well as значит «а также, наряду с» и, в отличие от and, ставит второй член на второе место по важности.
