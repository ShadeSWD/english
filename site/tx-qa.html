Качество и контроль
quality management and inspection: the plan, the tolerance and the record

Текст

THE QUALITY MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

Quality in shipbuilding is not the result of a final inspection; it is built into every operation. A modern yard therefore works under a quality management system certified to ISO 9001. The system describes who does what, in which order and with which documents. For each block the yard issues an inspection and test plan, which lists every check from the marking of the plates to the pressure test of a tank and shows who has to be present. Some of these checks are hold points: the next operation may not begin before the check has been signed. Quality is measured not by the number of defects found, but by the number that never appear.

Глагол build into («закладывать во что-то») в страдательном залоге сохраняет предлог: quality is built into every operation. После describes идут косвенные вопросы: who does what, in which order — порядок слов в них прямой, вспомогательный does не появляется. Конструкция not by … but by … противопоставляет два способа измерения; предлог повторяется в обеих частях.

ACCURACY AND TOLERANCES

The hull is built from thousands of parts cut by machine, and every part carries an error. Errors add up along a dimensional chain: if ten sections are joined one after another, the deviation of the last butt is the sum of ten small deviations. For this reason the yard works to a table of tolerances, in which every kind of joint has its own permissible deviation: a few millimetres for the gap in a butt, a fraction of a degree for the angle of a bulkhead. Where an error can accumulate, the parts are cut with an extra margin, and the exact edge is marked and trimmed on the berth.

Фразовый глагол add up значит «складываться, накапливаться». В условном предложении первого типа после if стоит настоящее время, а не будущее: if ten sections are joined, the deviation is the sum. Оборот a table in which every kind of joint has its own deviation — снова предлог перед which. Обратите внимание и на work to a table: предлог to здесь значит «по, в соответствии с».

NON-DESTRUCTIVE TESTING

A weld can look perfect and still hide a defect, so important welds go through non-destructive testing, that is examination without cutting the metal. Visual inspection comes first and finds most of the surface faults. Ultrasonic testing sends a beam of high-frequency sound into the metal and shows an echo from anything that breaks the beam. Radiographic testing places a source on one side of the joint and a film on the other, and the picture shows pores and slag as dark spots. Penetrant testing makes fine surface cracks visible by means of a coloured liquid that is drawn into them, and magnetic particle testing finds the same cracks by the way iron powder gathers above them.

Один модальный глагол работает сразу на два инфинитива: a weld can look perfect and still hide a defect — второе can не нужно. После предлога without глагол стоит в форме на -ing: without cutting the metal. Конструкция make something visible — «сделать что-то видимым»: после make идёт дополнение, а за ним прилагательное без глагола-связки. Сочетание by the way iron powder gathers значит «по тому, как собирается порошок».

TESTS, THE SURVEYOR AND THE RECORDS

Compartments are tested for tightness as soon as they are closed. A hose test throws a jet of water at the seam from the other side; a vacuum box shows a leak as bubbles in a soapy film; a hydrostatic test fills the tank with water to a head given in the Rules. The surveyor of the classification society attends the main tests and signs the reports. If something does not meet the requirement, a non-conformity is raised, and it is closed only after the corrective action has been carried out and verified. All these papers — reports, certificates, as-built drawings — are handed over to the owner together with the ship.

Союз as soon as («как только») требует настоящего времени, даже когда речь о будущем. Глагол throw строится с предлогом at, когда струю направляют на предмет. Сочетание meet the requirement повторяет уже знакомое meet the Rules. И запомните фразовый глагол hand over to — «передавать кому-либо»: в страдательном залоге оба слова остаются при глаголе.
