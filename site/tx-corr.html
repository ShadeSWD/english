Текст

WHY STEEL CORRODES

Corrosion of steel in sea water is an electrochemical process. Wherever the metal is wet, a corrosion cell is formed. At the anode the iron dissolves and passes into the water as ions; at the cathode the oxygen dissolved in the water is reduced; the sea water itself acts as the electrolyte and carries the current between the two areas. The anode and the cathode may lie a few micrometres apart on the same plate, or they may be two different metals joined together. Two metals in contact form a galvanic couple, and the less noble of them corrodes first. This is why a bronze propeller is dangerous for the steel around it.

Союз wherever («везде, где») вводит придаточное места и не требует после себя that. Причастный оборот the oxygen dissolved in the water стоит после существительного и переводится определением «растворённый в воде кислород»; сравните с личной формой the iron dissolves, где тот же корень уже глагол. Сочетание the less noble of them — сравнительная степень с указанием на группу: «менее благородный из них». Оборот this is why вводит следствие.

FORMS OF CORROSION

Corrosion does not always look the same. General corrosion attacks the whole surface evenly and is the easiest form to allow for. Pitting is far more dangerous: the total loss of metal is small, but a deep pit can go through a plate while the rest of the surface is still bright. Galvanic corrosion appears where two metals are joined, crevice corrosion in narrow gaps under washers and in lap joints, where the water stands still and the oxygen is used up. Corrosion fatigue is the worst of all: a crack that would need millions of cycles in dry air grows quickly in sea water, because every fresh surface at the tip of the crack corrodes at once.

Оборот the easiest form to allow for — инфинитив с предлогом на конце: предлог остаётся при глаголе allow for («учитывать»), хотя дополнение ушло вперёд. Усилитель far перед сравнительной степенью значит «намного»: far more dangerous. Во второй половине абзаца сказуемое опущено: crevice corrosion in narrow gaps — здесь подразумевается то же appears. Форма would need обозначает не прошедшее время, а предположение: «потребовала бы».

SURFACE PREPARATION AND PAINT

No paint holds on dirty steel, so surface preparation decides the life of the coating. Mill scale, rust, salt and oil are removed by abrasive blasting, and the cleaned steel is covered with a shop primer before it goes to the workshops. The paint system is then built up layer by layer, and its quality is measured by the dry film thickness: for ballast tanks the Rules ask for a nominal thickness of about 320 micrometres. Below the waterline the last layer is an antifouling paint. Fouling by weed and shells increases the resistance of the hull and therefore the fuel consumption, and modern self-polishing paints release their biocide slowly during the whole docking interval.

Глагол hold здесь непереходный: no paint holds on dirty steel — «краска не держится на грязной стали». Разные предлоги при близких глаголах надо запоминать парами: removed by blasting (средство), но covered with a primer (чем покрыто). Сочетание ask for в языке правил значит «требовать», причём предлог обязателен. Наречный оборот layer by layer строится без артиклей, как one by one или step by step.

CATHODIC PROTECTION AND THE CORROSION ALLOWANCE

Paint is never perfect, so the underwater hull is protected electrically as well. In the simplest method blocks of zinc or aluminium are welded to the shell. These sacrificial anodes are less noble than steel, so they corrode instead of it and are renewed at every docking. In impressed current cathodic protection a rectifier feeds a small current to permanent anodes, and a reference electrode keeps the potential of the hull within safe limits. Finally, the designer adds a corrosion allowance to the calculated thickness of every plate, so that the structure still meets the Rules at the end of the service life.

Наречие as well стоит в самом конце предложения и значит «тоже, вдобавок». Сочетание instead of it требует местоимения в объектном падеже. Придаточное цели вводится через so that с личной формой глагола; не путайте его с so в значении «поэтому», как в первом предложении абзаца. И запомните глагол meet в техническом значении: to meet the Rules — «отвечать требованиям Правил».
