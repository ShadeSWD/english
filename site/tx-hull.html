Текст

THE HULL AS A GIRDER

A ship is a beam that floats. Along the length of the hull the forces of weight and the forces of buoyancy are never equal in every section, and the difference bends the hull as a whole. Naval architects therefore treat the hull as a hollow box girder, which is called the hull girder. Its flanges are the upper deck and the bottom, and its webs are the two sides. The bending moment that acts on this girder consists of two parts: the still-water moment, which depends on the way the cargo is distributed along the ship, and the wave moment, which depends on the height and the length of the wave. When the crest of the wave is amidships, the hull hogs; when the crest is at the ends, the hull sags. The Rules of a classification society give the minimum section modulus of the midship section, and the designer is not allowed to go below it.

Заметьте два разных придаточных. Оборот с which отделён запятой и добавляет сведения, без которых предложение остаётся верным (the wave moment, which depends on the height of the wave). Оборот с that запятой не отделяется и сужает значение (the bending moment that acts on this girder — не всякий момент, а именно этот). Безличное which is called the hull girder — стандартный способ ввести термин: по-русски «который называют корпусной балкой».

FRAMING SYSTEMS

The plating alone cannot carry these loads, so it is supported by a framework of beams which is called the framing. Two systems are in use. In the transverse system the main members run across the ship: frames at the sides, floors at the bottom and beams under the deck. They are usually spaced from 600 to 800 millimetres apart. In the longitudinal system the main members run along the ship and are supported by deep web frames placed every third or fourth spacing. The choice between the two systems is not a matter of taste. Longitudinal framing gives a larger section modulus for the same weight of steel and is used where the longitudinal strength governs, that is, in the midship region and in long ships. Transverse framing is simpler to build and is kept in the ends, where local loads and the shape of the hull matter more. Most modern ships are built to a combined system.

Сочетание that is в середине предложения значит «то есть» и всегда выделяется запятыми. Глагол to govern здесь употреблён без дополнения: where the longitudinal strength governs — «там, где определяющей оказывается общая прочность». Обратите внимание на to be spaced: в технической прозе это обычный способ сказать «поставлены с шагом».

PLATING, STIFFENERS AND BRACKETS

Every surface of the hull — the bottom, the sides and the decks — is a grillage: a field of plating stiffened by two families of beams. The plating is welded from strakes. The strake at the upper edge of the side is the sheerstrake, and it is usually thicker than the others, because the stress in the hull girder reaches its maximum at the flanges. Ordinary stiffeners rest on primary members — girders, stringers and web frames — and the primary members rest on the transverse bulkheads. Wherever a beam comes to an end, its load has to be carried into the next member, and this is done by a bracket. Openings, cut-outs and hatch corners are the weak points of any structure, because the lines of stress have to flow around them. For this reason the corners are rounded and the edges are reinforced. A sharp corner in a highly stressed deck is the classical starting point of a fatigue crack.

Оборот has to be carried — модальный глагол долженствования плюс страдательный инфинитив: «нагрузка должна быть передана». Слово wherever («везде, где») вводит придаточное места; такие союзы с -ever в правилах встречаются постоянно. И запомните конструкцию this is done by a bracket: по-русски мы скажем «это делает кница», по-английски исполнитель уходит в конец после by.
