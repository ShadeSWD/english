English is an official language of more than 50 countries, and more than 500 million people speak it as their first or second language. If you can speak it too, you can communicate with them (both face to face and online). Whatever you are interested in* – science, music, sports, politics – you can find the information about it on the Web, and most Web pages are in English. English lets you feel the culture of other countries. You can watch British and American films and read books in the original. Learning English makes travelling easy because airports and train stations in many countries use it. Also, people who work in hotels usually speak English. 80% of the world’s research and academic papers* nowadays are in English. It is the language of most international conferences.
