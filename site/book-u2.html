Slava Orlov is a student at Saint Petersburg State Marine Technical University. He is doing a course in robotics at the faculty of Marine Electronics and Control Systems. He is in his first year. ___1___. He goes to the University every day except* Thursday and

Slava’s working day starts early. He gets up at seven o’clock, takes a shower, then he has breakfast. ___2___; he just has some sandwiches or eggs with a cup of tea or coffee. As he doesn’t live near the University, Slava leaves home at a quarter to eight. It takes him half an hour* to get to the university by trolley-bus. ___3___, or sometimes looks through his notes. The first double period begins at half past eight. At the university Slava attends lectures, classes and seminars. He doesn’t like to miss them. He also works in laboratories. Slava studies many subjects: physics and mathematics, descriptive geometry, computer science, history of shipbuilding and foreign languages. ___4___. Maths comes easy to him, but he is not very good at English. He usually has three or four double periods a day, and they are over at three o’clock. After classes he sometimes stays at the University to work in the reading-room, or to take part in a meeting of the Students’ Scientific Society.* ___5___ who live in the hall of residence. Besides,* on Wednesdays and Saturdays he has his training classes in rowing*. He lives a very full and busy life*.
