Юнит 6. Peter’s great creation
страдательный залог и Future Simple — и город, построенный ради моря

Коротко

  • Грамматика: страдательный залог в Present Simple (is / are + причастие) и Past Simple (was / were + причастие), деятель с предлогом by и орудие с предлогом with; Future Simple в действительном залоге (will, won’t, вопрос) и в страдательном (will be + причастие).
  • Тема: Санкт-Петербург — Петропавловская крепость, Летний сад, Адмиралтейская верфь и первый русский опытовый бассейн, морская промышленность и культура города.
  • Этикет: просьба и разрешение — can, could, will, would, may и вежливый отказ.
  • Слова: город и его памятники, верфь, типы судов, отрасли промышленности, даты и века.

Грамматика

1. Страдательный залог: что он делает с предложением

В действительном залоге подлежащее само выполняет действие. В страдательном залоге подлежащее действие испытывает. Прямое дополнение действительного предложения становится подлежащим страдательного, а прежнее подлежащее либо исчезает, либо ставится после предлога by.

Действительный залогСтрадательный залог
Peter the Great chose the place for the fortress. The place for the fortress was chosen by Peter the Great.
Trezzini built the cathedral. The cathedral was built by Trezzini.
The yard launches two tankers every year. Two tankers are launched every year.

2. Формы страдательного залога

Страдательный залог состоит из глагола to be в нужном времени и причастия второго (третья форма глагола). Меняется только to be, причастие остаётся неизменным.

ВремяУтверждениеОтрицаниеВопрос
Present SimpleThe hull is welded. The hull is not welded.Is the hull welded?
Present Simple, мн. ч.The plates are cut. The plates are not cut.Are the plates cut?
Past SimpleThe city was founded in 1703. The city was not founded then.When was the city founded?
Past Simple, мн. ч.The statues were brought from Italy. The statues were not made here.Where were the statues made?
Предлог by вводит деятеля — того, кто выполнил действие; предлог with вводит орудие или материал: The park was designed by a French architect, но The entrance is decorated with a portico. По-русски и то и другое часто передаётся творительным падежом, и в этом главная ошибка.

Страдательный залог особенно нужен в техническом тексте: важен результат, а не исполнитель.

The keel is laid in the building dock. The sections are joined by welding, and the seams are checked with ultrasound. The first Russian towing tank was opened in 1894, and the tests were carried out under the supervision of Alexey Krylov.

3. Future Simple в действительном залоге

Будущее простое время образуется вспомогательным глаголом will и инфинитивом без to для всех лиц. Отрицание — will not = won’t, вопрос — перестановкой will и подлежащего.

Our group will visit the Naval Museum next week. I will not be late. Will you take part in the conference? — Yes, I will. He won’t finish the drawing today, but he will finish it on Friday.

4. Future Simple в страдательном залоге

Формула проста: will be + причастие второе.

A new block of flats will be built near the yard. The report will be sent tomorrow. The bridge will not be repaired this year. When will the ship be delivered to the owner?
ВремяДействительный залогСтрадательный залог
Present SimpleThey test the model.The model is tested.
Past SimpleThey tested the model.The model was tested.
Future SimpleThey will test the model.The model will be tested.

Текст

THE CITY THAT WAS BUILT FOR THE SEA

Saint Petersburg was founded in 1703, during the Northern War, and it was founded for one clear purpose: Russia needed a port on the Baltic Sea. The place for the first fortress was chosen by Peter the Great himself — a small piece of land called Hare Island, where the river is wide and the approach can be controlled. Inside the walls the cathedral was designed by Domenico Trezzini, and its gilded spire, one hundred and twenty-two metres high, is still one of the symbols of the city. Later the cathedral became the burial place of the Romanovs. The Mint was placed in the same fortress; for many years part of the fortress was used as a political prison, and today the whole ensemble is used as a museum. Not far from the fortress the Summer Garden was laid out. A small summer palace was built there for the tsar, marble statues were brought from Italy, fountains were fed from a small river, and that is why the river is called the Fontanka. The famous iron railing was made in the eighteenth century and is still admired by everybody who walks along the Neva. The sea was always the main business of the city. The Admiralty shipyard was started in 1704, and warships for the young Russian fleet were built there. In 1712 the capital was moved from Moscow to Saint Petersburg. Timber and other materials were kept in the district which is known as New Holland. In 1894 the first Russian towing tank was opened, and the model tests were made under the supervision of Alexey Krylov; today his name is given to the research centre. The Academy of Sciences was founded here too, and Mikhail Lomonosov worked in it. The marine tradition is alive. About eighty per cent of the shipbuilding research institutes of the country and about thirty per cent of its shipyards are situated in Saint Petersburg. Tankers, icebreakers and submarines are designed and built here. Power machinery, general machinery, electronics and instruments are also produced in the city. The city was renamed twice and returned to its first name in 1991. During the Second World War it survived the siege of Leningrad, and that memory is kept by every family. Its historic centre is included in the UNESCO World Heritage list, and the city is often called the cultural capital of Russia. New ships will be launched here, new bridges will be built, and the students of our university will work on them.

Формы could и would вежливее, чем can и will: их выбирают с незнакомым человеком и в деловом разговоре. Глагол may в просьбе о разрешении звучит официально и уместен, например, при входе в кабинет. Отказ почти всегда смягчается оборотом I’m afraid …
