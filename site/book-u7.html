SIZE MATTERSРазмер имеет значение

If we break a piece of bulk material, for example, a piece of gold, into a hundred smaller parts, it won’t result* in any change of physical or chemical properties – in the macroscopic world these properties do not depend on the size. But if we break our piece of bulk material into particles whose dimensions are smaller than 100nm, we’ll see a very different picture. In the nanoworld, size is everything. For example, gold particles larger than 10nm melt at the same temperature as bulk gold – 1064°C. However, as particles become smaller, the melting point goes down, and 3nm particles melt at 300°C. Also, silicon particles of about 50nm in size show the same optical properties as bulk silicon, but 1020nm particles absorb ultraviolet light completely, which bulk silicon never does. While bulk copper is a popular material for wires, 60nm copper particles do not conduct electricity at all. Aluminium nanoparticles can burn, and carbon ones are water soluble. Many other materials also become very different from their macroscopic form.
