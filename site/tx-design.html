Текст

THE OWNER’S REQUIREMENT

A ship begins as a sentence in a business plan: so many tonnes of cargo, over such a distance, at such a speed. Out of that sentence the designer has to obtain a set of main dimensions, a hull form, an arrangement of the spaces and a power for the machinery. The document which fixes the demands of the owner is the specification, and everything that follows it is a compromise between the lines of that document. Speed costs power, power costs fuel, and fuel costs deadweight. A wider ship is more stable but slower; a higher freeboard is safer but raises the centre of gravity. None of these demands can be satisfied in full at the same time, and the task of the designer is not to find the perfect ship but to find the best possible one for a given service.

Отрицательное подлежащее none of these demands требует сказуемого без второго отрицания: по-русски мы говорим «ни одно… не может», по-английски «не» ставится только один раз. Обратите внимание и на пару not … but: the task is not to find the perfect ship but the best possible ship — «задача не в том, чтобы найти…, а в том, чтобы…». Обе части после not … but должны иметь одинаковую форму.

THE DESIGN SPIRAL

Because the requirements act against each other, the design cannot be produced in one pass. It is developed in cycles, and this way of working is known as the design spiral. In the first cycle everything is estimated roughly, usually from a similar ship — a parent vessel whose service is close to the service required. The dimensions are then checked against the weight equation: the displacement has to be equal to the sum of the light weight and the deadweight. If the sum does not balance, the dimensions are corrected and the cycle is repeated. Each turn of the spiral uses better data than the turn before it, and the naval architect moves from an estimate based on statistics to a calculation based on drawings. The spiral ends when the changes from one turn to the next become smaller than the accuracy of the data itself.

Слово whose относится не только к людям: a vessel whose service is close — «судно, характер работы которого близок». Оборот is known as вводит принятое название, в отличие от is called, которое просто называет. И запомните to be checked against: «проверяться по чему-либо, сверяться с чем-либо» — предлог against, а не by.

FORM, ARRANGEMENT AND THE RULES

Two things are settled in parallel with the dimensions: the hull form and the general arrangement. The form is described by coefficients — the block coefficient, the prismatic coefficient and the midship coefficient — and each of them influences the resistance in its own way. A full ship carries more cargo per metre of length, but it needs more power for the same speed. The general arrangement drawing shows where the cargo, the machinery, the tanks and the crew are placed; it decides the stability, the trim and the safety of the ship as much as the lines do. From the very first cycle the designer also has to keep the Rules in view: subdivision, freeboard, the criteria of stability and, in recent years, the index of energy efficiency. A design which does not comply with them cannot be approved, however elegant it may be.

Сравнительный оборот as much as the lines do кончается заменителем сказуемого do — так избегают повтора глагола decide. Уступительное however elegant it may be («каким бы изящным он ни был») строится по формуле however + прилагательное + подлежащее + may be. И различайте comply with (соответствовать требованиям) и correspond to (соответствовать чему-то по смыслу): в правилах употребляется только первое.
