Судовые системы
shipboard systems: the pipes, the pumps and the Rules

В описании систем предложение обычно строится по одной схеме: «система делает то-то, ведя жидкость оттуда-то туда-то». Поэтому здесь много глаголов движения и много предлогов направления — именно они, а не термины, чаще всего и мешают понять фразу с первого раза.

Текст

SYSTEMS, PIPES AND FITTINGS

A shipboard system is a set of pipes, fittings, valves, pumps and tanks that moves a liquid or a gas from one place in the ship to another. Systems are usually grouped by what they carry: bilge and ballast water, fuel oil, lubricating oil, cooling water, fresh water, sewage, compressed air and steam. The pipes are made of steel, copper or a plastic approved by the classification society, and they are joined by welding or by bolted flanges. Between the pipes stand the fittings: shut-off valves, non-return valves that let the liquid pass in one direction only, strainers that hold back dirt, and sounding pipes through which the level in a tank is measured.

Сочетание grouped by what they carry показывает, что после предлога может стоять целое придаточное. В обороте let the liquid pass инфинитив идёт без частицы to — так же ведёт себя make, а после help возможны оба варианта. Конструкция pipes through which the level is measured ставит предлог перед which; в разговорной речи его унесли бы в конец, но в правилах и описаниях принят именно такой, книжный порядок.

THE BILGE AND BALLAST SYSTEMS

The bilge system removes the water that collects in the lowest part of each compartment. A suction is led to every hold and every machinery space, strainers protect the pump, and non-return valves prevent the water from running back into a space that is already dry. The Rules fix the internal diameter of the main bilge line by a formula that contains the length, the breadth and the depth of the ship, and they require at least two independent power pumps. The ballast system works with clean sea water: it fills and empties the double bottom and the side tanks in order to keep the draught, the trim and the stability of the ship within the limits of the loading manual.

Глагол prevent требует схемы «prevent + кого/что + from + герундий»: prevent the water from running back. Сочетание at least («не менее») в правилах встречается постоянно и всегда стоит перед числом. Оборот in order to keep — цель; в отличие от простого to keep, он подчёркивает намерение и удобен в длинном предложении, где иначе неясно, к чему относится инфинитив.

FUEL, OIL AND COOLING

The fuel oil system carries oil from the bunker tanks through the settling tank and the purifier to the main engine. Heavy fuel has to be heated before it can be pumped, and the steam heating coils in the tanks are therefore part of the system. The lubricating oil system takes oil from the sump tank, cools it, filters it and delivers it to the bearings under pressure. Cooling is done in two circuits: sea water is taken in through a sea chest and passes through the central cooler, while fresh water circulates in a closed circuit around the cylinders. The closed circuit keeps salt away from the engine.

Два модальных значения подряд: has to be heated («нужно подогреть») и can be pumped («можно откачать») — оба со страдательным инфинитивом. Союз while здесь не «пока», а «тогда как»: он противопоставляет два контура. Наконец, keeps salt away from the engine — модель «keep + дополнение + наречие»: «не пускает соль к двигателю».

FIRE-FIGHTING, SANITARY SYSTEMS AND PUMPS

The fire main runs along the whole ship and feeds the hydrants; each hydrant carries a hose and a nozzle, and two jets of water must reach any point of the deck. The Rules again require two fire pumps, and one of them must be driven independently of the main engine. The sanitary system supplies fresh and sea water to the accommodation, and sewage — black water from the toilets and grey water from the showers — goes to a treatment plant before it is discharged overboard. Every pump is chosen for two figures: the capacity in cubic metres per hour and the head in metres, that is the height to which it can raise the liquid.

Наречие independently of требует предлога of, а не from. Вставка между тире поясняет слово sewage и по смыслу равна нашему двоеточию. Глагол supply строится двояко: supply water to the accommodation и supply the accommodation with water — смысл один, предлоги разные. В конце снова that is с пояснением, а the height to which it can raise — тот же книжный порядок с предлогом перед which.
