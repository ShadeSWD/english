Текст

SIZE, DEVIATION AND TOLERANCE

No part can be made exactly to its nominal size. The drawing therefore gives not one figure but a field: a nominal size and two limit deviations, the upper one and the lower one. The difference between the maximum and the minimum limits of size is the tolerance, and it is always a positive number. The wider the tolerance, the cheaper the part, so the designer is expected to specify a close tolerance only where the function demands it. In the ISO system the tolerances are standardised in grades, from IT01 to IT18. The same grade means the same relative accuracy over a whole range of sizes, because the tolerance unit grows together with the size of the part: a shaft of sixty millimetres in grade IT7 is allowed thirty micrometres, while at six millimetres the same grade allows only twelve.

Конструкция the wider …, the cheaper … — двойная сравнительная степень: «чем шире…, тем дешевле…». Артикль the здесь обязателен в обеих частях, а глагол-связка часто опускается. Обратите внимание на is expected to specify: сложное подлежащее, по-русски — «от проектанта ожидается, что он назначит». И различайте deviation (отклонение от номинала, со знаком) и tolerance (допуск, всегда положительный).

FITS AND THE HOLE-BASIS SYSTEM

When two parts are assembled, what matters is not the size of each of them but the relation between them, which is called the fit. If the hole is always larger than the shaft, the fit is a clearance fit and the parts can move relative to each other. If the shaft is always larger than the hole, the fit is an interference fit and the parts are held together by the elastic forces in the material. Between these two lies the transition fit, in which either a small clearance or a small interference may occur. In order to reduce the number of tools, the required fit is normally obtained by changing the shaft and keeping the hole the same; this is the hole-basis system, and the basic hole is designated H. The designation 60 H7/g6 is therefore read as a hole and a shaft of the same nominal size with different fields of tolerance.

Оборот what matters is not A but B — способ выделить главное: «важен не A, а B». Придаточное с предлогом in which either a small clearance or a small interference may occur устроено так же, как в тексте о сварке. Заметьте порядок слов в between these two lies the transition fit: после обстоятельства в начале предложения подлежащее и сказуемое меняются местами — это книжная инверсия, обычная в описаниях.

SURFACE, GEOMETRY AND THE CHAIN

Two parts may lie within their tolerances and still not fit, because a size says nothing about form. For this reason the drawing also carries geometrical tolerances: flatness, straightness, roundness, parallelism, perpendicularity and position, each of them referred to a datum. Roughness is specified separately, most often as the arithmetical mean deviation Ra in micrometres. In shipbuilding, where the parts are large and are welded rather than machined, the tolerances are given in the standard for hull work: the deviation of a plate edge, the gap in a joint before welding, the misalignment of two plates. Errors add up along a dimensional chain, and a chain of six links, each of them within its own tolerance, can still produce a section which does not fit the next one. This is why the yard measures during the assembly and not only at the end of it.

Сочетание rather than значит «а не», «вместо того чтобы»: welded rather than machined — «сварные, а не обработанные резанием». Оборот each of them referred to a datum — причастный оборот с собственным подлежащим: по-русски «причём каждый из них отнесён к базе». И запомните to add up — «складываться, накапливаться»: об ошибках и допусках говорят именно так.
