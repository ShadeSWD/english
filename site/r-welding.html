Текст

WHY SHIPS ARE WELDED

Until the middle of the twentieth century the plates of a hull were joined by rivets. A riveted seam needs overlapping plates, thousands of holes and a heavy angle bar along every joint. Welding replaced riveting for two simple reasons. First, a welded hull is continuous: the metal of one plate becomes the metal of the next one, and the joint is as strong and as watertight as the plate itself. Second, a welded structure is lighter, because there are no overlaps and no rivet heads. Ten per cent less steel in the hull means ten per cent more cargo for the same displacement. Welding is also faster than riveting and much of it can be done by a machine. Today almost every joint in a hull, from the keel to the funnel, is welded, and riveting survives only in a few special places.

Обратите внимание на пару weld — welded — welding: один корень даёт глагол, причастие и название процесса. Сравнение строится оборотом as strong and as watertight as the plate itself — «такой же прочный и такой же непроницаемый, как сам лист». Предлог by в joined by rivets и done by a machine указывает на средство выполнения работы.

THE ARC AND THE JOINT

In arc welding an electric arc burns between the electrode and the work. The arc is very hot: it melts the edges of the parts and the end of the electrode at the same time. The melted metal fills the gap and forms a weld, while the coating of the electrode gives a gas and a layer of slag which protect the hot metal from the air. When the metal cools, the two parts become one piece. Engineers describe a weld by the type of the joint, by the penetration and by the position. Two plates that lie in the same plane give a butt joint. A plate that stands on another plate gives a tee joint, and the weld in the corner is called a fillet weld. Full penetration means that the weld goes through the whole thickness of the metal. A weld in the flat position is easy; an overhead weld is slow and difficult, and for that reason the yard turns the section whenever it can.

Здесь важен порядок слов в определениях: arc welding, butt joint, fillet weld — первое существительное работает как прилагательное и не имеет окончания множественного числа. Заметьте также придаточные с that и which: two plates that lie in the same plane. По-русски это причастный оборот, по-английски — целое придаточное предложение с собственным сказуемым.

DEFECTS, INSPECTION AND DISTORTION

No process is perfect. A weld can contain cracks, gas pores, slag inclusions or a lack of penetration at the root. Such defects reduce the strength of the structure, and after a few years at sea a small defect can start a fatigue crack. Every important weld is therefore checked. The welder and the foreman look at the surface first; then the yard uses ultrasonic testing for internal defects and radiography for the most responsible seams. The surveyor of the classification society reads the reports and signs the results. Heat brings a second problem. The weld metal shrinks when it cools, and the plate bends towards the weld. This distortion is reduced by a correct sequence of welds, by rigid clamps and jigs, and by welding the two sides of a joint one after the other. Deformations that remain after the work are removed by careful local heating, which the yard calls flame straightening.

Названия дефектов почти все — существительные без артикля во множественном числе: cracks, pores, inclusions. Исключение — a lack of penetration: «отсутствие проплавления», здесь артикль нужен. И запомните строй последнего абзаца: is reduced by … , by … and by … — способ действия перечисляется одинаковыми предложными группами.
