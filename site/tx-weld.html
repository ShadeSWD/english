Текст

PROCESSES AND CONSUMABLES

A modern yard does not use one welding process but a whole family of them, and each of them has its place. Manual metal arc welding with a covered electrode is flexible and needs almost no equipment, so it is still used for repairs, for short seams and in positions which a machine cannot reach. Gas metal arc welding, in which a wire is fed continuously through the torch and the arc is shielded by a gas, gives a much higher deposition rate and is the main process in the panel shop. Submerged arc welding, where the arc burns under a layer of flux, is the fastest of the three, but it can be carried out in the flat position only. Whatever the process, the consumables must match the parent metal. An electrode that is too strong for the steel is just as dangerous as one that is too weak.

Обратите внимание на предлог перед which: in which a wire is fed — «в котором проволока подаётся». По-русски предлог тоже стоит перед союзным словом, но в английском он часто уезжает в конец (positions which a machine cannot reach = positions that a machine cannot reach). Оборот whatever the process — сокращённое уступительное предложение: «каким бы ни был способ». Полная форма была бы whatever the process may be.

HEAT INPUT AND THE HEAT-AFFECTED ZONE

Welding is a local metallurgical process. The energy which the arc puts into the joint per unit length of the seam is called the heat input, and it is calculated from the current, the arc voltage and the travel speed. The heat input controls the cooling rate, and the cooling rate controls the structure of the metal next to the weld, that is, the heat-affected zone. If the metal cools too quickly, a hard and brittle structure is formed and cold cracks may appear, especially when hydrogen is present in the joint. This is why steels with a high carbon equivalent are preheated before welding and why the interpass temperature is kept within stated limits. If the metal cools too slowly, the grain grows and the toughness of the joint falls. The welder therefore works inside a narrow window, and the boundaries of that window are given in the welding procedure.

Здесь два условных предложения первого типа: if the metal cools …, a structure is formed. После if в английском стоит настоящее время, хотя речь о будущем результате; будущее время после if — типичная ошибка. Сочетание this is why вводит следствие («вот почему»), а не причину; причину вводит this is because. И заметьте within stated limits: причастие stated здесь значит «оговорённый, установленный документом».

PROCEDURES AND QUALIFICATION

No responsible seam is welded by improvisation. Before production starts, the yard writes a welding procedure specification, which states the process, the preparation of the edges, the consumables, the range of the current, the position, the preheat and the interpass temperature. A test piece is then welded under these conditions and examined: the weld is radiographed, specimens are cut out and tested in tension and in bending, and the toughness is measured on notched specimens. If the results satisfy the Rules, the surveyor approves the procedure and it becomes qualified. The welders themselves are qualified separately, for a range of positions and thicknesses. Records are kept for every seam, so that years later it is possible to find out who welded the joint, with which consumable and under which procedure. Traceability of this kind is what turns a set of good intentions into a quality system.

Оборот so that … it is possible to find out вводит цель; в отличие от русского «чтобы», после so that стоит полное предложение с подлежащим и сказуемым. Косвенные вопросы who welded the joint, with which consumable идут в прямом порядке слов — без вспомогательного did. И запомните формулу what turns A into B: «то, что превращает A в B»; what здесь заменяет целое подлежащее.
