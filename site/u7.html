Грамматика

1. Условные предложения первого типа и придаточные времени

Условие первого типа описывает реальную возможность. Главное предложение стоит в Future Simple, а придаточное — в Present Simple, хотя по-русски в обеих частях будущее время. Точно так же ведут себя придаточные времени с союзами when, while, as soon as, till / until, after, before.

Придаточное (Present Simple)Главное (Future Simple)
If we heat the sample,its properties will change.
When he graduates,my brother will go into industry.
As soon as the test is over,we will send you the results.
We will waittill the coating dries.
If we break a piece of gold into smaller parts, the properties won’t change. But if the parts become smaller than one hundred nanometres, the metal will behave in a new way. Before the model is tested, it will be measured again. After the particles are added, the composite will become much stronger.
Частая ошибка — сказать «if we will heat». После if и после союзов времени will не ставится. Исключение — вопрос о будущем, где when вводит не придаточное времени, а дополнение: I don’t know when the sensor will be ready.

2. Модальные глаголы must, mustn’t, needn’t, should

ГлаголЗначениеПример
mustдолг, необходимость, сильный совет You must read the safety rules before you enter the workshop.
mustn’tзапрет You mustn’t touch the sensor with your hand.
needn’tотсутствие необходимости You needn’t repeat the test: the first result is reliable.
shouldсовет, рекомендация You should compare your data with the standard.
shouldn’tсовет не делать We shouldn’t ignore the effect of nanoparticles on the environment.
Различайте mustn’t и needn’t: первое — «нельзя», второе — «можно не». You mustn’t open the valve значит «открывать запрещено»; You needn’t open the valve значит «открывать не обязательно». После всех модальных глаголов инфинитив стоит без to, а окончания -s в третьем лице нет.

3. Пассивный инфинитив после модальных глаголов

Если подлежащее само действие не выполняет, после модального глагола ставится be + причастие второе.

Действительный залогСтрадательный залог
We can do this work in a week.This work can be done in a week.
You must protect the sensor from dust.The sensor must be protected from dust.
Engineers should check the surface twice.The surface should be checked twice.
Nobody can measure such a particle with a ruler.Such a particle cannot be measured with a ruler.

5. Связки причины и следствия и предлоги

Научный текст держится на связках. Причину вводят as, because и since; следствие — so, thus, that’s why, as a result.

As the particle is very small, most of its atoms lie on the surface. The surface is very active, so the material burns easily. Copper particles of sixty nanometres stop conducting current; that’s why they cannot be used in a wire. Silicon absorbs ultraviolet light, and as a result the coating protects the skin.

Текст

WHY SMALL PARTICLES BEHAVE IN A NEW WAY

If you cut a piece of copper into two parts, both parts will be copper. You can repeat the operation many times and nothing will change. But as soon as the particles become smaller than one hundred nanometres, the properties begin to depend on the size, and the familiar material becomes almost unfamiliar. Ordinary gold melts at one thousand and sixty-four degrees Celsius, while a gold particle of three nanometres melts at about three hundred degrees. Silicon particles of ten to twenty nanometres absorb ultraviolet light completely. Copper particles of sixty nanometres stop conducting current. Aluminium, a safe structural metal, burns when it is ground into nanopowder, and carbon, which is not soluble in water, becomes soluble in this form. Why does it happen? Three reasons should be named. First, on this scale quantum effects appear, and the electrons are limited in their motion, so the energy of the material changes. Second, gravity is very small compared with electromagnetic forces, and thus the behaviour of the particle is controlled by its surface, not by its weight. Third, the particle is smaller than the wavelength of visible light, so it cannot reflect that light in the usual way. As a result, the share of the atoms that lie on the surface grows very quickly, and a surface atom is always more active than an atom inside. Engineers use these effects in three ways. Particles can be sintered into a solid body: such steel is about fifty per cent stronger than usual, armour ceramics become harder, and a ceramic lens can even be made transparent. Particles can be added to a base material: polymer composites become stiffer, zinc and titanium oxides protect the surface from ultraviolet light, and silver works as an antibacterial addition. Finally, particles can be put on a surface as a thin layer, and then the surface becomes anti-reflective, anti-fogging or self-cleaning. The new world is not free of risk. Nobody knows exactly what will happen when large amounts of nanoparticles get into water and soil, so the effect on the environment must be studied before such materials are produced on a large scale. Engineers should be careful, and safety rules mustn’t be ignored.
Вопросы к тексту

1. At what size do the properties of a material begin to depend on the size of a particle? 2. Compare the melting point of ordinary gold and of a gold particle of three nanometres. 3. What three reasons for the new behaviour are named in the text? 4. What are the three ways of using nanoparticles? 5. Why must the effect on the environment be studied?

В ответах пользуйтесь связками as, so, thus, that’s why, as a result и пассивным инфинитивом после модальных глаголов.

Прощание в английском разговоре состоит из трёх шагов: предупреждение об уходе (I’m afraid I must be going), ответ собеседника (Well, I won’t keep you then) и пожелание (Have a nice evening). Уходить сразу после «good-bye» считается невежливым.
